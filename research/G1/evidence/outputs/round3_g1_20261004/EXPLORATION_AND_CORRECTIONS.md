# Development paths, errors, and excluded evidence

All changes are under the new round3 directory. No development count is an independent statistical N.

1. `raw/pair_exploration*` and `raw/pair_r*` are reconnaissance only. Their continuous indistinguishability horizon was located by a mesh and rounded to control cells, so they are **not valid final controller-independent lower certificates**. They returned no useful separation; no main claim relies on them.
2. `raw/frequency_exploration*` and `raw/freq_eta*` are a discarded fixed-quantizer adversarial experiment. Its first run included phase `5.00001`, outside the nominal `(0,5]` family; the code was subsequently corrected, but the original result files are retained and **excluded from scientific claims**. Fixed quantizer bins were also a weak adversary because unnecessarily crossing a quantizer boundary reveals information.
3. `adversarial_frequency_outer.py` replaces that attempt with nested bounded-diameter clusters and valid `(0,5]` phases. It is an exploratory finite-world lower relaxation, not an exact optimal sensor-capacity solution. At noise .19 it reproduces a weaker coarse lower .41476; at smaller errors it did not separate. The exact whole-family opacity theorem and interval-audited continuum lower are the final evidence.
4. `power_tree.py` attempted a large conventional robust LP with .025 control cells, .1 samples and .05 delay (320,706 variables). It was deliberately interrupted after the directly validated conventional predictive filter supplied a smaller, simpler constructive witness. No optimum, infeasibility, or runtime-complexity conclusion is reported from the interrupted run. CLI now rejects observation deliveries not aligned with control cells.
5. The first independent feedback extremum implementation used a derivative where the exponential-affine coefficient was required. Dense replay caught the underestimated peak. The corrected formula, all reruns, failed test log and endpoint-safe final policies are retained in `feedback_baseline/`. The final independent interval checker uses its own global exponential-polynomial implementation.
6. The interval checker originally discarded tiny enclosed events and skipped tiny A intervals straddling zero. Independent review rejected these shortcuts. It now retains all nonexact-zero merged events and encloses the full time cell whenever A straddles zero. All final certificates were rerun.
7. An early round2 audit hash differed for one proof file because round2 itself had a documented 2026-10-03 expository correction. The final round2 writer manifest and its correction ledger resolve this. All 127 files in the final writer manifest match, and all 12 classical-baseline manifest entries match. This was not a round3 modification.
8. Network transfer rejects its first sparse-phase fitted controller because untrained phases violated the command limit. The final design enumerates observation-equivalence classes and verifies the remaining continuous phase uncertainty. The refined universal network no-feedback lower does not separate from the feedback upper; no universal network resource theorem is asserted.

The interrupted and excluded branches are disclosed to distinguish research progress from post-hoc success selection. They should not be counted as additional positive experiments.
