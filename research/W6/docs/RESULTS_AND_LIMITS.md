# Research question, findings and limits

## Research object and model

W6 studies a single useful job with random completion workload, a known power-to-service law, controlled **actual** dynamic power, slew limits, and a physically accounted recovery to the reference state after completion. The objective combines energy and cycle time; it is not automatically a regenerative average-power ratio. Power-law service is a model restriction. Instantaneous EOS visibility, available post-EOS burn, redirectable useful work, state closure, and no exogenous reward for wasteful work are substantive assumptions, not features demonstrated on a real GPU.

The mathematical papers and engineering route must be evaluated separately. The former can stand only with explicit contracts and a careful novelty comparison. The latter needs a measured actuator/service/quality/electrical chain that this payload does not supply.

## Findings that survive current review

1. Full-cycle return reserves admit an exact terminal recovery projection under the stated assumptions. Power-law service and a sharp dimensionless tail-hazard condition recover a convex residual problem. Nonzero initial states require prepaid recovery or a forced descent prefix; arbitrary distributions/services and hard deadlines are outside the main theorem.
2. Finite atomic EOS admits an exact bridge reduction and Bellman lower/upper constructions. Exact serialized-path algebraic feasibility is distinct from floating objective evaluation. The twelve saved inward primary witnesses have exact rational constraint checks; objective/dual gaps remain ordinary floating evaluations.
3. There are 12 primary parameter configurations, 10 scaling-equivalence classes, 12 distribution-transfer targets, 6 nonzero-initial configurations, 8 synthetic atomic configurations and 2 empirical-length-law configurations. Counts are **configurations, not independent deployment samples**.
4. The safe-primary relative dual gaps are approximately 8.70e-8–3.14e-6; synthetic atomic gaps about 0.0124%–0.0458%. The two Azure-law gaps are about 0.0746% and 0.3327%. None is an outward-rounded cost certificate.
5. Weighted cycle-objective reductions versus the corrected global constant-target class are 0.198%–1.694%. True matched-cycle comparisons exist for six configurations, giving dynamic-energy reductions of 0.455%–3.866%; six other constant-target matches cannot achieve the proposed latency. Do not call a weighted-objective change an energy saving, or infer dominance over every possible controller.
6. W6's signed grid-memory extension shows why a zero-state terminal projection cannot simply be carried over to a signed network response. Sufficient fixed actual-power caps and amplitude/slew support bounds are provided; the exact signed-grid optimal policy class is not solved. The primary guard study has 1 design + 8 frozen confirmations, 72 modeled executor combinations, and a separately disclosed knee-alignment amendment. Peaks often occur after power return; tail accounting is essential.
7. Four inherited linear-network transfers are exploratory; a high-gain negative baseline result is retained. The qualified ANDES 2.0.0 reduced matrix, adapter source, source-case hash and GPL notice are included. This is a generated linear benchmark, not an identified facility model; full ANDES regeneration requires the upstream qualified release.

Primary sources: `research/full_cycle/FINAL_SCIENTIFIC_STATUS.json`, `research/full_cycle/theory/FULL_CYCLE_THEOREMS.md`, `research/full_cycle/audit/`, `research/grid_memory/FINAL_THEORY_GRID_STATUS.json`, and `research/novelty/GATE_DECISION.json`.

## Negative results and repairs that must remain visible

- The old hard-deadline candidate failed; later full-cycle theory does not reverse it
- Round-2 saved-trajectory accounting was not universally correct: 33 of 288 budget paths needed derived correction; old unconditional saved-path claims are withdrawn
- A flat target search understated a stronger baseline; the old maximum 6.85% claim is withdrawn, not relabeled as the new result
- Nonzero-state and accounting failures/intermediate outputs remain under `research/full_cycle/evidence/` with the repair ledger
- Signed grid-memory projection and nonconvexity counterexamples, support-witness repairs, and the high-gain transfer negative are retained
- Broad novelty gate fails; the narrower combined recovery/hazard theorem remains conditional, with moderate model-specific technical increment rather than a new general control method

## Latest quality/cost continuation

Historical rescoring has 108 requests but only three distinct MAIN output hashes, repeated 24 times each. Writing fails an explicit five-sentence condition. Code has fenced output, five functions rather than one, missing examples, and `find_runs([1,2,3])` produces `[[1,2]]`. The arithmetic case has four correct final answers; no complete quality certification is inferred. All natural-EOS claims are based on host metadata, not an independent token/device-time attestation.

`continuation/quality/LEGACY_RESCORE.json` preserves every request-level result. Four safe original output texts (three MAIN cases plus one warmup), a 108-request denominator/index and allowlisted terminal metadata are included in deduplicated form; `rescore_compact.py` reproduces the quality diagnostics offline. Repeated large host-observation artifacts are not redistributed; the original 324 required file hashes and an explicit import recipe are retained. This release does not claim that omitted raw bytes were freshly re-audited during publication preparation.

The new synthetic benchmark has 18 tasks, 9 structural families, and development/calibration/heldout splits of 6 tasks and 3 families each. It is a small author-written pilot. Its family split is not population random sampling, contamination-proof blind testing, or an external benchmark. Reference fixtures and 288 code differential cases validate the validator only.

Writing requires blinded human semantic/genre review after the machine gate. Math checks only specified exact answers/check equations. Code supports a bounded AST subset; unknown syntax is unknown, never excluded from the denominator. The AST interpreter is not a general secure Python execution sandbox. Model output is never passed to Python `exec`/`eval`.

## Unsupported statements

This package does not establish measured same-quality energy savings, real cap tracking, actual-power control, a measured service curve, burn availability, full thermal/regenerative closure, concurrency support, DC/PCC calibration or mapping, nonlinear network validity, a regulatory frequency budget, strict numerical certification, or paper readiness. `J_W6` stays null. A reproducibility pass only certifies the particular checks it ran.
