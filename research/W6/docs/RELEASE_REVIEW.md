# Public release review

## Prepared content and precedence

This is a filtered, portable research handoff, not a claim of paper readiness or completed hardware validation. The 2026-10-07 CPU quality continuation takes precedence over earlier quality assumptions. The conditional native-vLLM graph/eager comparator is a **plan**, not an observed result and not physical cap/control novelty. The README, results/limits, experiment roadmap and starting prompt state the evidence levels explicitly.

## Included deliberately

- Authored W6 theory, source, frozen model outputs, independent audit source, failures, repairs and numerical limits
- Small licensed Azure length metadata, attribution, full CC BY notice and verified raw hash
- Only the qualified reduced Kundur benchmark component needed by W6, its adapter/export source, GPL notice, model-specific provenance and generated W6 transfer kernels
- Four safe deduplicated historical output texts, a 108-request denominator/index and allowlisted terminal metadata, sufficient for offline reproduction of fail-closed quality diagnostics; no claim that repeated outputs increase quality N
- Dry-run acquisition source, schemas and intentionally unauthorized operator templates, alongside a clear live-operation gate

## Excluded deliberately

- The unrelated grid-forming project, original broader archive, private working notes, upload/save receipts, credentials, machine environments, user contact details and private infrastructure paths
- Repeated multi-megabyte host observations, original detailed inference identity/boot/timing metadata and original archive ZIPs
- Models/weights, installed environments, caches, wheels, dependencies and raw paper PDFs; figures are authored/generated research outputs
- Obsolete whole-folder manifests that do not describe the filtered public payload

The compact quality index is a derived allowlisted artifact. Its original-source hashes do not turn it into complete raw telemetry or independent device/token validation. Its test replays diagnostics only. Original input verification remains optional and requires separately obtained authorized inputs.

## Remaining public-release risks

1. **Authored-code license decision:** no blanket outbound license was established in the source. Do not invent one; confirm rights-holder publication authority and desired reuse terms. Azure and GPL-covered components retain their own notices and conditions
2. **Scientific overclaim:** historical reports contain snapshot-specific wording and hashes. Current top-level limits govern; broad novelty, same-quality energy savings, real control, DC/PCC validity and paper readiness remain unestablished
3. **Hardware safety:** acquisition/cap source can operate hardware only through explicit live flags and reviewed configuration. Example values are placeholders, not recommendations. Automated tests exercise CPU mocks/dry-run paths, not live hardware
4. **Benchmark exposure:** public heldout examples and fixtures are visible and cannot support an unseen/contamination-proof claim after inspection. Writing requires blinded human review
5. **Security limits:** the bounded AST interpreter is not a general Python sandbox. Do not expand generated-code execution or remote connectivity without a separately reviewed design and authorization
6. **Reproduction boundary:** saved numerical replay differs from fresh full-model, nonlinear/source-model or physical-experiment regeneration. Requirements are recorded versions, not a complete cross-platform lock
7. **Historical-source access:** original raw inference archives may not remain public; standalone compact quality reproduction avoids depending on them but does not validate original timing/energy traces

## Integrity and sanitation

`provenance/SOURCE_FILE_MAP.json` records original and publication hashes. Any path sanitation, portability adjustment or checksum-sidecar update is identified by changed bytes. Original scientific arrays/negative results are retained; no favorable numerical values were substituted. `PUBLIC_MANIFEST.json` and `MANIFEST.sha256` define this payload; historical per-report hashes are provenance only.

`verification/CPU_PUBLICATION_CHECKS.json` records the newly executed CPU tests and their exact scope. `verification/RELEASE_SCAN.json` records a bounded file/content scan; it is not a formal privacy/security proof. Independent release review belongs to the final publisher. Nothing here authorizes publishing or pushing.
