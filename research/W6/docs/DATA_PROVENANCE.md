## Other sources and hash semantics

Literature ledgers contain bibliographic/source links and historically observed access limits; raw copyrighted PDFs are excluded. NLR/DaRUS/ML.ENERGY candidates are coverage discussions, not copied matched datasets. No access gate was bypassed.

`MANIFEST.sha256` covers current publication bytes, excluding itself. `provenance/SOURCE_FILE_MAP.json` maps original artifact-relative identifiers/hashes to publication hashes. Historical hashes in reports identify original snapshots and do not certify this sanitized/filtered tree. Obsolete whole-folder manifests were excluded. Protocol checksum sidecars are regenerated if sanitation changes their file, with original hashes retained in the source map. Integrity does not establish licensing, quality, hardware validity or scientific correctness.
