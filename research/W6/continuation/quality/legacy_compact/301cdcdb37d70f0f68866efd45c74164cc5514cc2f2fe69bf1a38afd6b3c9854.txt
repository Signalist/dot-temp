### Problem 1: Arranging Books on a Shelf

**Problem Statement:**
A library has 120 books. If the librarian arranges the books on a shelf such that each shelf can hold 10 books, how many shelves are needed?

**Solution:**
1. **Total Books:** 120
2. **Books per Shelf:** 10
3. **Number of Shelves Needed:** 
   \[
   \text{Number of Shelves} = \frac{\text{Total Books}}{\text{Books per Shelf}} = \frac{120}{10} = 12
   \]

**Intermediate Calculations:**
- **Division:** 
  \[
  120 \div 10 = 12
  \]

**Final Answer:**
The librarian needs 12 shelves to arrange all 120 books.

**Consistency Check:**
- **Total Books:** 120
- **Books per Shelf:** 10
- **Number of Shelves Needed:** 12
- **Consistency Check:** The solution is consistent with the problem statement.
