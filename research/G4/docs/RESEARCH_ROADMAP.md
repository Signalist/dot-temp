# G4 research roadmap and paper gate

## Candidate contribution and paper structure

The candidate is an infinite-horizon-to-one-block capacity equivalence under a lossy common-output task contract, followed by the DAG slot-swap characterization and assumption boundaries. Structure a narrow theory paper around (1) admissible task languages/information/common initial state; (2) B ≥ B*_S + W∞ and optimum B*_S; (3) graph components and connected prefix-count formula; (4) exact finite certificates and scenario-LP equivalence; (5) restricted history, discrete retention and action-set boundaries; (6) calibrated-computing limitations. The candidate is conditional until theorem priority is resolved.

## First gate: precise prior-art subtraction

Audit the closest storage-privacy and scheduling-polytope results, rather than searching only computing-energy terminology. Use the 18-record primary-source ledger and exact section locators. Arrieta–Esnaola–Effros already covers ideal arbitrary-word-family common-output geometry; Avula covers storage losses/self-discharge; Felsner–Reuter and Naatz cover linear-extension graph foundations; Ćustić–Klinz covers constant objectives; robust inventory/reachability/LP/Cesàro arguments are classical. Resolve what remains in the lossy infinite nonperiodic equivalence and DAG contraction. Record inaccessible full text explicitly. If the combined theorem is already implied, reduce to an application/synthesis or stop the novelty claim.

## Minimum next experiments and baselines

1. Independently verify all 89 rational primary/transfer certificates with the stdlib checker. Confirm primal feasibility, dual signs/balance, equal objectives, and zero gap; do not infer exactness of floating leakage results
2. Regenerate the same 77 primary and 12 transfer configurations. Compare with a direct all-allowed-word LP using identical information, compact action polytope, losses, word set, initial-state choice and reset. Expected result is equal optimum, not a win
3. Reproduce the six-task same-multiset capacities 6080/187, 3800/187, 3040/187. Report each allowed word language; do not change task multiset, workload energy or information unnoticed. Quantify structural description/compression separately from optimizer speed
4. Ablate DAG edges/labels and connected components while controlling the permitted language; identify redundant graph constructions that project to the same H/L words. Repeat ideal-efficiency and step-bound examples but report that their 10 records are outside the 89-checker coverage unless a new independent checker is built
5. Reproduce the finite-horizon trend, positive-width equality witness, private-initialization failure, nonconvex-action cycle, support-restricted alternating histories and disposal counterexample. Probabilities with identical support must not be called a capacity improvement
6. Reproduce the discrete-retention invariant-interval grid and threshold ηcηd ≤ ρ^(N−1); distinguish exact rational witnesses from floating LP agreements

## Transfer and engineering branch

- Task Bench-shaped predecessor relations are mathematical graph transfers with synthetic H/L power. No Task Bench runtime or measured power trace is currently used
- Hold out graph families and task counts by preregistered generation rules. Evaluate semantics and scaling without presenting graph instances as independent hardware samples
- A real parallel workload branch needs measured per-task duration/power, permitted concurrency, communication/barrier cost, scheduling throughput and energy, and synchronized PCC/storage measurements. Unequal durations and concurrency can invalidate equal-slot serial proofs; formulate a new theorem/model first
- Continuous leakage requires weighted within-slot propagation, not copying the discrete theorem. Converter slew, delay, power limits, imperfect measurement and terminal recovery must be modeled explicitly before UPS/PCS claims
- Historical grid-transfer code concerns measurement/hidden-state examples, not proof of real source attribution. Five prerequisite matrices are withheld; do not quietly use arbitrary replacement grid models

## Completion and stopping criteria

Continue a narrow theory draft only after the residual theorem has a documented closest-prior comparison, exact certificates reproduce, counterexamples are preserved and every computational claim uses a matched LP. Stop optimizer-superiority framing because the current baseline is equal. Stop high-originality framing if the theorem is classical containment. Stop deployment/throughput/attribution framing until physical calibration and the required model extensions are validated. Additional synthetic configurations do not clear these gates.
