# Arithmetic correction before accepting any experiment
2026-10-02: independent code audit found that point-valued mpmath transcendental approximations expanded by one binary64 ULP do not enclose mathematical zero at quadrantal angles. No result from that implementation is accepted. Replaced pi/sin/cos construction with mpmath.iv at 80 decimal digits followed by outward endpoint rounding to binary64. Also clamped a sum-of-squares lower bound to zero before sqrt (outward addition may otherwise produce a negative subnormal at exact zero). Initial run/log is preserved as invalid numerical development. Model contracts, seeds and gates unchanged; all accepted certificates rerun from scratch with corrected arithmetic.
