# Stopping criteria and permissions

This handoff supports local reading, non-destructive analysis and reproduction in disposable copies of the G3 evidence. It does not itself authorize publication, submission, external messages, paid downloads, cloud-compute purchases, hardware control, access to private datasets, credential changes or license selection. Obtain the owner's specific approval before any such action. Never bypass paywalls or access restrictions.

A research continuation should stop at a decision-bearing result: a verified reproduction/mismatch, a precise antecedent containment or residual theorem, a violated contract assumption, or a defined measurement/access blocker. Preserve failures, parameter changes, frozen source hashes and scientific-result hashes. Do not overwrite evidence or silently change tolerances to produce success.

Publication gates: clear included-material rights; decide the original-code license with its owner; ensure the repository audience is authorized; verify no private data/credentials/third-party full text; substantiate the narrowed claim and closest-prior comparison. The candidate is not certified paper-ready. The root public manifest validates file integrity, not scientific correctness, legal rights or novelty.

Do not upload raw papers or datasets simply because they were available for reading. Sources with unverified redistribution terms stay as attribution, retrieval instructions and hashes. Access/license failure is an explicit blocker, not permission to find an unauthorized mirror. Use only ordinary public sources or separately authorized access.
