# G3 research roadmap and paper gate

## Candidate contribution and paper structure

The publishable candidate is the all-allocation prefix exclusion coupled to a same-contract sampled-port inner witness. A paper would organize: (1) continuing-work/port/service/recovery contract; (2) energy identity and support inequality; (3) the frozen outward outer certificate; (4) exact held-command construction and correction; (5) matched baselines and mechanism ablations; (6) workload energy ledger; (7) implementation and priority limits. Do not present convexity, Fubini, support functions, quadratic completion, LP, SOCP or a smaller runtime as independent novelty.

## First gate: closest full-text containment

Before expanding experiments, compare every assumption, quantified control class, prefix energy term and recovery requirement against the September 2026 Jaramillo/Carrion/Aguila Tellez energy-headroom work in the ledger; its direct full text was unavailable in the frozen search. Compare static output convexity (Streitmatter), service/recovery flexibility (Evans), reachability (Johnson–Hauser), and polynomial nonnegativity (Xia–Alizadeh). Produce a theorem-by-theorem matrix with exact source locators, covered result, remaining distinction, and access uncertainty. A bounded search failing to find an antecedent is not priority clearance. If the coupled prefix theorem is already contained, downgrade to application/reproducibility rather than cosmetically rename it.

## Reproduction and smallest discriminating experiments

1. Recheck the frozen 812.5 outward intervals, exact payload, 9603 rational profile comparisons, failed 50/100 μs attempts, and endpoint repair. Preserve old artifacts and record new hashes
2. Rebuild LP grids 25/50/100 μs with unchanged d(t), parameters, initial delay, and final recovery; independently certify only candidates satisfying every hard constraint. Negative inner LP margin is not an outer impossibility proof
3. Rebuild continuous CLARABEL SOCP at 808 under the original permission set. Report held-vs-continuous actuation explicitly. A solver benchmark must separately compare identical parameterization, information, tolerances and action sets
4. Rerun fixed-test-function mechanism ablations: full prefix deficit + inductive release; deficit removed; old global release plus shared budget. A negative test gap means the chosen certificate fails, not physical feasibility
5. Recompute same-pointwise-workload S versus feasible no-service L ledgers for signed, positive, absolute AC increment and endpoint W/B/Z changes. Keep 100 J net cap separate from 173.156281 J signed increment, 14,352.563644 J positive and 28,531.971007 J absolute increments. L is not an optimal no-service counterfactual

## Transfers and implementation work only after the first gate

- Outer transfer: document the whole ordered slower-port box by comparison proof, then independent numeric checks as debugging. Do not replace the comparison theorem with corner sampling
- New workload transfer: predefine continuous d(t), service window and recovery contract; match service/no-service input pointwise and keep task completion unchanged. Report the changed contract, not pooled sample counts
- PWM: use the same 808 contract, explicit 10 kHz bridge, command-hold schedule, finite tracking tolerances, controller state, post-0.2 s behavior and energy ledger. A relaxation of instantaneous cap/floor or exact reset creates a new contract. Preserve negative results
- Robust inner: first determine whether feedback, parameter adaptation or finite-tolerance restoration is permitted. The common finite-horizon open-loop exact reset for a continuum of pure-lag parameters has an obstruction. Corner success is insufficient
- Measured transfer requires synchronized actual power, task progress/completion, controller command, DC/battery state, phase voltage/current and recovery, with documented calibration and access permission. Synthetic waveforms cannot establish throughput or real-system benefit

## Completion and stopping criteria

A narrow draft can proceed only with a precise residual contribution after prior-art review, unchanged-contract independently reproducible certificates, honest matched baseline, failed cases and all model limitations. Stop the high-originality branch if the new theorem is contained by prior work. Stop any implementation claim if required actuation/tolerances cannot be matched, all-time constraints fail, or synchronized measurements are unavailable. Do not continue many same-source sweeps as a substitute for these gates. A smaller bracket alone is not a hardware rating or paper acceptance claim.
